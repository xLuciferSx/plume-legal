# Plume Support
_Help for Plume: AI Email on iPhone._

**Contact:** Questions, bug reports and feature requests: [open an issue](https://github.com/xLuciferSx/plume-legal/issues/new). We usually reply within two working days.

## Adding an account
Tap your provider on the welcome screen. Outlook, Hotmail and Microsoft 365 accounts sign in on Microsoft's own page; Plume never sees your password. For iCloud, Yahoo and other IMAP providers, use an app-specific password from your provider's security settings.

## Removing an account
Open Settings, tap the account and choose Sign out. Plume removes that account's sign-in details and the mail stored for it on your iPhone. Your mail stays untouched on your provider's servers.

## AI features
Summaries, reply ideas, sorting, translation, event and bill detection are written by Plume AI. Turn off automatic sorting in Settings > Filtering. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for how email text is processed.

## Undo Send
Every email waits a few seconds before it is sent, with an Undo button at the top of the inbox. Change the delay in Settings.

## Notifications
Plume alerts you only when a person writes to you, never for newsletters or promotions. You can limit alerts to VIPs or turn them off in Settings.
